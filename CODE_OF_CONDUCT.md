# Code of conduct

Genesis follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and constructive, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and sharing someone's private
information are not acceptable, here or in any space that represents the project.

The rules apply in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space,
and to everyone, maintainers included.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, [@marktiderman](https://github.com/marktiderman), through GitHub. Reports
are kept confidential. The maintainer decides on a response, from a private warning to a ban,
following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
