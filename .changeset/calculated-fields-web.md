A renderer for the `calculated` field type: the worked-out value, read-only, in an `<output>` named by the question and announced politely as it changes. Numbers, percents and text follow the field's `format` and `decimals`; with no value yet it shows a dash. It is in `builtInRenderers` and `defaultRegistry`.
