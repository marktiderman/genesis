A view can describe its calendar: `defineView(contract, { calendar: { field, endField, allDayField, laneBy, layout, weeks, weekStartsOn } })`, checked by `defineView` and read by every platform. ResourceView gains a calendar mode: `<ResourceView view={v} mode="calendar" />` places a view's rows by a date or timestamp field in a month grid, 1 to 4 weeks, a printable day-by-day agenda, or one lane per person. It asks the provider for the rows that overlap the visible window (long events that began earlier included), inside the view's scope and filters and under core's list keys so saves refresh it, and says "+N more" past its 500-row cap and in busy day cells; lands timestamps on the field's display clock (its own zone, the scope's or the viewer's), with `timestamp without time zone` read as wall time; gives events with no lane an Unassigned lane; shows an event that spans days on each day, all-day events first; draws a dot per person (named for screen readers); moves between days with the arrow keys, Home/End and PageUp/PageDown, each day read with its event count; and opens the list's peek on click (or `calendar.onSelect`). `EventCalendar` and its placement helpers (`calendarWindow`, `placeEvents`, `readEventTime`, `windowBounds`…) are exported from `/data` for events that aren't one view's rows. `readEventTime` (stored dates and timestamps as calendar values) is in `genesis-core/dates`. Studio's month calendar is now drawn by `EventCalendar` and fetches the whole grid it shows.
