`/signed`: `createSignedImageUrls(signer, { base, derivatives, items, maxItems?, path?, sign? })` signs every declared derivative (ADR 0012 names: `color/800`, `bw/2400`, `web-1080`, `poster`) of the given items and resolves to an `ImageUrlBuilder` for `responsiveImage`. Every URL is signed for its own derivative (`mediaScope.file`), hour-aligned by default (`sign` is laid over that default, so `{ ttl: "3h" }` stays aligned) so `srcset`s cache. It signs and never decides access. The builder carries the derivative `widths`, which `responsiveImage` uses in place of its `widths` option, so each `srcset` `w` is the derivative's own and an original narrower than the widest derivative is never labelled with its own width; and `expires`, the earliest link expiry, to cap the HTML's `cache-control` or ISR `revalidate` below. One call signs at most `maxItems` items (default `DEFAULT_MAX_SIGNED_ITEMS`, 1,000): sign one page at a time. Two derivatives at one width, and a derivative named `original`, are refused. `mediaSrc` refuses ids the handler would refuse (a slash, backslash, NUL or percent-escape) instead of signing links that are always 403. Also `finishSizes` and `parseSignedImagePath` (for the handler's `route`).

`ImageUrlBuilder` may carry `widths`: a builder of fixed files (signed derivatives) whose widths `responsiveImage` offers as they are, and `srcsetWidths` takes `{ fixed: true }` for that.
