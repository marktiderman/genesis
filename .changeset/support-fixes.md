Features: support-feedback, support-helpdesk
Three migrations, numbered after #801's 0013 and 0014 (0015 is taken by a follow-up). `0016_genesis_support_ticket_fixes.sql` (with support, after 0004) moves the Data Studio's audit log, comments, outbox and dead-letter rows (and the outbox payloads' resource) from `public.support_reports` and `public.support_attachments` to `public.tickets` and `public.ticket_attachments` where those tables exist, and adds `support_agent_unread_count(tenant)` (`client.agentUnreadCount`). `0017_genesis_support_help_answers.sql` (with support-helpdesk, after 0010) clears a help question's answer and cited articles when it ends `safety_routed` or `no_consent`. `0018_genesis_support_agent_queue.sql` (with support-chat, after 0013's queues) adds `support_agent_queue(tenant, inbox_view)`, the agent queue with no row cap of its own, so the provider pages and counts it, with 0013's views and meanings (`mine` and `unassigned` hold active tickets, then `pending`, `closed`, `all`) and its `waiting_since`; `support_agent_unread_count` counts active tickets only, so a pending one doesn't show in the badge; `ticketSegments` gains Pending and Closed (Solved is now Closed); `supportQueueSource({ tenant })` reads it and passes the tenant (the view's `tenant_id` scope filter, or the one given, which still takes the list's `tenant_id` out of the query). The README says how an app's own reply notifications tell the verify prompt apart: `support_replies.prompt = 'verify'`, never the reply's words.
