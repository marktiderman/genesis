Starter themes: whole-app looks built on a brand, one per kind of work. Seven ship (`tracker`, `repository`, `checklist`, `notebook`, `press`, `journal`, `arena`), each a brand plus the sidebar, card, font stacks and shell density that give a product its character, and each reaching WCAG AA on every pair genesis-ui draws, light and dark. Use them as is through `themes/starter.css` and a `data-theme` attribute on `<html>` or any element, or make your own with `defineStarterTheme` (`@marktiderman/genesis-design-system/starter-themes`): `starterThemeCss`, `starterThemeVariables` and `starterThemeContrastProblems`.
