Native chat review fixes. The thread list is upright and oldest first: FlashList in its bottom-anchored chat mode (FlashList 2 never read the `autoscrollToTopThreshold` the inverted list relied on, so a new message could land off screen), FlatList following new messages by hand, both going to the bottom when you send, and VoiceOver reading each day's heading and the "New messages" line before their messages. A bubble with media carries its hidden controls as VoiceOver actions (play or pause a voice note, change its speed, open each photo). A push reply goes through the outbox (the live one, or the saved one when the chat screens aren't mounted), so an offline reply waits with Retry, and its client id comes from the notification and `data.messageId` (or the text), so two replies under one collapsed notification never collide. The voice recorder shows a notice when its stop fails or leaves no file, and ends cleanly (with a notice, nothing sent) when the app goes to the background or a call or another app takes the microphone; leaving the screen while a stop is under way lets it finish. Drafts are kept per signed-in person (`draftKey(userId, conversationId, threadRootId)`, `useChatComposer(conversationId, threadRootId, userId)`), `clearDrafts` removes one person's on sign-out, and the composer starts empty when the screen moves to another conversation. TUS uploads read a file a chunk at a time through the new `openFile` option, and a 409 on create counts as done only when the object is really there.
