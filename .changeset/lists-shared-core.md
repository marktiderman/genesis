Features: view-filters, view-actions, saved-views, resource-view-board, resource-view-calendar, resource-view-sheet, view-inbox
One list for the web and the phone (ADR 0017), and the review of the lists release:

- **Core holds the logic, each platform draws.** `useListState` (segment, declared filters, type, grouping, a saved view's search, sort and columns; the URL both ways, text filters after a pause, no storage read while rendering, filters on the viewer's clock, `snapshot` for saving) and `useSegmentCounts`. `useBoardColumns`, `useViewBoard` (with each platform's `words`), `useViewCalendarRange`, the calendar placement (`calendarWindow`, `shiftAnchor`, `placeEvents`, `eventDays`, `calendarLanes`, `eventDots`), `timeStorage`, `windowBounds`, `displayZone` and `calendarEvents` (a view's rows as events) move to core; genesis-ui re-exports them under the same names. `useResource` takes `thenSort`, a second sort under the list's own.
- **Saved views per viewer.** `SavedViewStore.forViewer(id)`: the device store keeps each viewer's views and choices apart; lists use it. A server store scopes its rows to the viewer.
- **Filters.** A date range on a timestamp column is the viewer's whole days (`gte` the first day's start, `lt` the day after the last); a time with no offset is on their clock (`filterParams(values, zone)`). `filterValueFor` and `applicableListState` drop a malformed stored value rather than throw; `isFilterSet` takes anything.
- **Web.** Bulk Delete comes from ResourcePage's own delete (`bulkDelete`: the screen's `can`, the view's guard, one confirm step with the count, the recycle-bin wording), and isn't offered with `onDelete` or `rows`. The row menu's Edit and Delete go through `can` and show a refusal disabled (`ResourceRowMenuBuiltIn.refused`). A view's `groupBy` no longer groups a table on its own; a grouped list keeps `orderBy` as its second sort. While every matching row is selected, actions that take only loaded rows wait, with a reason. Totals over a server-paged list say they cover the loaded rows (`DataTable footerPartial`, `footerLabel`). Group headers keep unique keys; Shift ranges skip rows in closed groups. The inbox's reading pane offers Edit and Delete only where `can` and the view's guard allow, words its check boxes from the message catalogue, and counts only the picks still shown. `BulkTarget` says a `matching` run checks access itself.
- **Sheet.** `type="sheet"` (`ResourceSheet`): a view's rows on SheetGrid, the view's writable fields editable on rows its guard and `can` allow, each change through the resource's update row by row, a refused or failed cell marked with its reason, and the last change undone. `SheetGridColumn.cellReadOnly(row)` makes one row's cell read-only with words.
- **Phone.** `NativeResourceView` holds core's list state: segments as chips with counts (`NativeViewSegments`), declared filters in a sheet (`NativeViewFilterControls`), the type switcher (`NativeViewTypeSwitcher`, `switcher`, `savedViews`), the view's own actions in each row's swipe and long-press menu with their reasons (`onAction`), `type="board"` (`NativeResourceBoard`, a long press moves a card) and `type="calendar"` (`NativeResourceCalendar`, agenda and month). `type="inbox"` needs the view's inbox block and runs its `markRead` on opening an unread row.
- **Chat.** `conversationInbox({ resolveUser, fallbackTitle })`: a direct conversation's title is the other member's name, and the preview is the latest message. An inbox block's `title`, `preview` and `from` take a function of the row.
