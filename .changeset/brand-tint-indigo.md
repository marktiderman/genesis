The default Genesis brand primary is indigo-600 (`#4f46e5`, `243 75% 59%`; 6.3:1 on white) instead of emerald, which was the same colour as `success`, so a primary button read as a success state. `--primary`, `--ring`, `--sidebar-primary`, the native `GENESIS_PRIMARY`, the brand preset's accent (dark: indigo-400) and the theme roles' default tint all follow. Apps with their own brand are unaffected; apps on the default brand change colour.

Features: design-tokens, theme-roles
