Design systems and an OKLCH colour engine. `defineDesignSystem` defines versioned look rules (`crisp@1`): a fluid type ramp (a `clamp()` per step), weights, tracking and case per font class, section density, corners, border and shadows, surfaces, image treatment, and CSS-only motion levels (none, calm, lively, bold). A brand names one with the new optional `CanonicalBrand.designSystem` (`{ id, density?, corners?, motion? }`), and `themeFromBrand(brand, { designSystem })` adds its `--ds-*` tokens to the variables, plus, for a design system with `surfaces`, colour roles from `palette()`: brand-tinted neutrals on a warm, cool, pure or night ground, the accent as a fill and as solved text, and every text pair solved to pass WCAG AA in light and dark. The output's new `designSystem` field carries the palette and the contrast pairs. Ships `classic@1` (no tokens: today's look, the default), `crisp@1` and `soft@1`, with `registerDesignSystem` / `getDesignSystem` for more. Brand lengths may now be fluid: `LENGTH_REGEX` (and the JSON Schema) accept `clamp(<rem>, <rem> + <n>vw, <rem>)` and nothing else, with min not above max; native reads the minimum. The font list (`FONT_CHOICES`, `SELF_HOSTED_FONTS`, with each font's class) moves here from genesis-sites, at the new `/fonts` entry; `/design-systems` is also a new entry. The package has a README.
