Print a calendar per person: `PrintableAgenda` prints one sheet per lane for a week (each lane's own events by day, every day listed, the app's `sections` under each week, a page break per sheet), and `PrintButton` opens the browser's print dialog and hides itself on the page. Messages `calendar.print` and `calendar.weekOf`.

Features: resource-view-calendar
