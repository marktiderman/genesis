Native chat: async video messages, link previews and app attachment cards. `ChatVideoRecorder` records in the thread on expo-camera (`modules.camera`): tap to start and tap to send, or hold and let go; flip between takes; the time against the role's limit; close to throw a take away; camera and microphone asked for when it opens. A recording goes through `ChatProvider videoUpload` (a Mux direct upload, minted when recording starts) in resumable chunks (`chunkedPut`, retried and resumed after a dropped connection), with progress and Retry above the composer (`VideoUploadTray`), then is sent through the outbox as a pending Mux attachment; without `videoUpload` it goes to storage as a file. `ChatVideoPlayer` plays HLS and files on expo-video (`modules.video`): poster and duration, tap to play, pause, mute, 1x / 1.5x / 2x, full screen, "Processing" until Mux is done, one at a time with voice notes. `ChatLinkPreviewCard` shows a link's site, title and description under its message (no preview for hidden messages; the image only with `linkPreviewImages`), opening in expo-web-browser when given (`modules.webBrowser`), else the system browser. `ChatAttachmentCard` draws `ChatProvider attachmentKinds` (a form, a check-in) with its button, or `ChatThreadScreen onOpenAttachment`. Genesis UI Native adds the icons `video`, `camera-flip`, `volume`, `volume-off`, `expand` and `stop`.
