The AI contract's "now" items, all additive. `AiContext` gains `actor` (`"person"` by default, `"agent"` or `"system"`), `runId` and `conversationId`; they go on the span (`genesis.ai.actor`, `genesis.ai.run_id`, `gen_ai.conversation.id`) and to the store's `begin`. `RunEvent` is a union named after AG-UI's events (`RUN_STARTED`, `TEXT_MESSAGE_*`, `TOOL_CALL_*`, `STATE_DELTA`, `RUN_FINISHED`, `RUN_ERROR`, and `CUSTOM` named `genesis.interrupt` for an approval pause); `createAiHandler` streams them with `stream: "events"` (`stream: true` is unchanged), `textStreamToRunEvents` converts today's stream, and `useAiStream` reads both. `AiStore` is split into ports (`ConsentStore`, `BudgetStore`, `UsageStore`, `KeyStore`) that it extends. `defineTask` stamps `spec: 1` (`AI_SPEC`) and refuses another spec. A wrapped processor's span is now named `{operation} {id}` (for example `moderation chat.moderate`), as OpenTelemetry's GenAI conventions name spans; it was `{id} {vendor}`.
