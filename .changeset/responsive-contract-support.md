`SupportMessengerLauncher` takes `variant="icon"`: an icon button named by its words, with the unread count as a badge on its corner. With `position="inline"` it is a ghost button for a header or toolbar; floating, it is a filled round button. On a phone with a bottom tab bar, put it in the header rather than float the launcher over the page (docs/RESPONSIVE.md, rule 9).

Its peer range on `@marktiderman/genesis-ui` now takes 3.x as well (`>=2.36.0 <4.0.0`).
