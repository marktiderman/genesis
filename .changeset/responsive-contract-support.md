`SupportMessengerLauncher` takes `variant="icon"`: a ghost icon button named by its words, with the unread count as a badge on its corner, for a header or a toolbar. On a phone with a bottom tab bar, put it in the header (`position="inline"`) rather than float the launcher over the page (docs/RESPONSIVE.md, rule 9).
