Settings: the tenant and person layers, and Realtime refresh (migration `0023_genesis_settings_tenant_person.sql`). `genesis_tenant_settings` stores a tenant's values for keys declared `scope: "tenant"`, set by that tenant's admins (`private.genesis_tenant_admin`) through `set_genesis_tenant_setting`; `genesis_person_settings` stores a person's own values for keys declared `scope: "person"`, through `set_genesis_person_setting`. Each write is checked against the synced catalogue, and refused for a key of another scope or one genesis.config.json or the database layer locks; every change goes to `genesis_scoped_setting_changes`. A tenant's members read its values (its admins alone the history), a person their own, platform admins everything. `genesis_settings_layers(tenant)` returns the tenant's layer and the signed-in person's; `readSettingsLayers` and `resolveAppSettings` take `tenantId` and resolve with them; `setGenesisSetting` and `clearGenesisSetting` take `layer: "tenant" | "person"`. Changes to any settings table are broadcast on private Realtime channels (the layer and key only), and the new `watchAppSettings` re-resolves on each. Until 0023 is applied, the client reads 0020's layers and resolves without tenant and person values, warning once (docs/MIGRATION.md).
