The catalog names the look-and-feel pieces, so `genesis agents` writes them into an app's guide (and `genesis catalog` lists them): custom views, view types of your own, record pages (open modes and the document template), widgets, the shell's navigation style and frame, DocumentPage, appearance preferences, and an organization's brand.

The TimePicker row names its `timeZone` prop and points at the README's TimePicker section, beside the rule for a time of day in a form; the calendar, board and gallery rows write `<ResourceView mode="…">` as code, so a rendered guide keeps it.
