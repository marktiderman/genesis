New `NativeChoiceCard`: one selectable option as a card (a radio, or a checkbox with `multiple`; a `media` slot for a thumbnail or icon; title and description; `invalid` for an unanswered question), read as one radio or checkbox by VoiceOver and TalkBack. `NativeInlineAlert` and `NativeBanner` take `children` (content under the description) and `actions` (a row of buttons). `NativeProgress` takes `indicatorColor` and `trackColor` as colour values, for a screen or package that styles without NativeWind. `NativeRating` matches the web `Rating`: `size="touch"` (a 44pt target per star), `tone="primary"` (the brand's tint), `clearable={false}` and `disabled`.
