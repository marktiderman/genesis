Sites render from the brand's design system. `SiteBrand` takes `designSystem` (`{ id, density?, corners?, motion? }`) and an optional `colors.secondary`; `siteTheme(brand, theme, { designSystem })` themes through genesis-design-system's `themeFromBrand`, uses the colour engine's roles and contrast pairs when the design system has surfaces, and reports `theme.designSystem`. Sections draw tokens: heading sizes from the fluid ramp (the hero can reach the design system's display size instead of stopping at 60px), heading weight, tracking and case by font class, eyebrows and lead copy, section padding by density, same-surface neighbours sharing their padding instead of doubling it, corners and button corners, a photo filter, the least wash over a photo that keeps text at 4.5:1 (instead of 80%), and a scroll-driven reveal below the fold that skips the hero and honours reduced motion. Each rule falls back to today's value, so `classic@1` (the default) renders exactly as before and keeps its release hash; the markup gains only `data-gs-size`, `data-gs-text`, `data-gs-spacing` and `data-gs-scrim` hooks and a tokenised button radius. Swapping design systems changes only the theme's variables, never the HTML. `/templates` adds the `forge@1` and `studio@1` design systems (`industryDesignSystems`, to register with `registerDesignSystem`) and `IndustryTemplate.designSystems`. `FONT_CHOICES` and the self-hosted font list now come from genesis-design-system (still exported here).
