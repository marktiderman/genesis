Chat on the web for migration 0002, with the core and hooks native shares. Core: `topics`, `setTopic`, `schedule`, `scheduled`, `cancelScheduled`, `availabilityFor`, `myAvailability`, `setAvailability`, `broadcast`, `search` and `linkPreviews`, `ChatMessage.moderation`, and pure helpers (`isWithinWorkingHours`, `awayLine`, `hoursSummary`, `extractLinks`, `scheduleQuickPicks`, `topicKey`). Hooks: `useTopics`, `useScheduled`, `useAvailability`, `useBroadcast`, `useSearch` (debounced), `useLinkPreviews`, and `useConversation().loadUntil`. Web: a topic bar, scheduled send from the send button with a list to cancel, an away banner and `ChatAvailabilitySettings`, `ChatBroadcast`, `ChatSearch` (in the thread and across the panel, jumping to the message), link preview cards (https images only, no HTML), moderation notices and badges, a full emoji picker (frimousse) for reactions and the composer, video recording and Mux HLS playback (`hls.js` optional), and `ChatAttachmentCard` for app kinds registered through `ChatProvider attachmentKinds`; `ChatProvider videoUpload` sends recordings through Mux direct uploads. Fixes: a message sent while the saved outbox loads is no longer lost; a thread catches up every time its live channel joins, the first time included; "Load earlier" works on a cached thread; a failed role check offers a retry (`accessError`, `refetchAccess`) instead of "can't post"; unsent messages retry once after loading and when the browser comes back online, with `useChat().retryAll()` for native; `useChat().clearOutbox()` empties the outbox on sign-out.
