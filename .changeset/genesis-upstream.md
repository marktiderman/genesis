`genesis upstream check` validates an app's register of what it needed from Genesis (`docs/genesis-upstream.md`: id, kind gap/extension/invention, status, feature, package, local, need) and warns when a promoted entry's local copy is still there. `genesis upstream export <id>` prints the link to Genesis's new gap issue form, filled in from the entry without its local path; it sends nothing.
